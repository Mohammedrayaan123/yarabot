SCHOOL CAMPUS
----------------------------------------------------------
The campus is a customized facility, previously a university building, located at Wazeer Street, Dheera District, Riyadh.
Total campus area: 9,890 square metres (2.44 acres)
Built-up area: 6,600 sq. metres
Play area: 3,290 sq. metres
The campus has two completely separate blocks for boys and girls, plus a dedicated academic block and administration office area.
Facilities include: spacious classrooms with Digital Teaching Systems, well-equipped Science laboratories, four Computer labs, a full library with reading room, Dance & Music rooms, a Health and Wellness facility, playgrounds, a KG recreation area, a canopy-roofed assembly area, a self-contained infirmary with a full-time nurse and medical aid, and a large parking lot for personal cars and school vehicles.

ADMISSION PROCEDURE
----------------------------------------------------------
Admission is open to Indian Nationals only, subject to seat availability. New academic year admissions are generally processed in the last quarter of the previous academic year; the Principal may grant exceptions under special circumstances.
 
Application forms are available from the school office for SR 50 (incl. VAT), Nursery to Grade XI, or downloadable from the website with payment at submission.
 
ENTRANCE TEST: Required for all fresh admissions from UKG upward. Non-refundable fee: SR 100 (incl. VAT).
- UKG: English and Mathematics (LKG syllabus)
- Grades I & II: English, Mathematics, Hindi
- Grades III to VIII: English, Mathematics, Hindi, 3rd Language
- Grades IX & X: English, Mathematics, Science, 2nd Language
- Grades XI & XII (Science PCM): English, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Grades XI & XII (Science PCB): English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology
- Grades XI & XII (Science PCMB): English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics
- Grades XI & XII (Commerce): English, Business Studies, Accountancy, Economics
- Grades XI & XII (Humanities): English, History, Sociology, Economics
 
Direct admission is given to Class X & XII, subject to CBSE requirements. Cut-off date for fresh admission to Class IX & XI is 30th June; for lower grades, 30th September.
 
CLASS XI STREAM ADMISSION CRITERIA:
Science Stream: minimum 60% in Science & Mathematics individually, aggregate 60% in Pre-Board and Periodic Tests, minimum 60% in Entrance Test.
Commerce Stream: minimum 60% in English & 50% in Mathematics individually, aggregate 60% in Pre-Board/PTs, minimum 60% in Entrance Test.
Humanities Stream: minimum 60% in English & 50% in Social Science individually, aggregate 60% in Pre-Board/PTs, minimum 60% in Entrance Test.
75% attendance is mandatory for Class XI and XII per CBSE Examination Byelaws.
 
REQUIRED ADMISSION DOCUMENTS:
Birth certificate, valid passport copies (student, father, mother), valid Iqama copies (student, father, mother), student's visa page copy, student's Aadhar card copy, original Transfer Certificate (with NOOR details for local transfers), previous report cards (Grade II and above), for Grade XI admission: PT1/PT2/PT3 and Pre-Board report cards attested by Principal, Bonafide Certificate of Grade X, financial clearance/No Dues Certificate from previous school, sponsor letter with CR Number, vaccination card copy (Nursery to Grade I admissions).
 
Transfer Certificates are valid for one month from issue date. TC issuance requires 7 working days notice and a fee of SR 200 (incl. VAT). TC cancellation after formal request costs SR 100 (incl. VAT) administrative charge.
Withdrawal mid-quarter requires full payment for that quarter (non-refundable).
Re-admission after removal from rolls requires SR 200 (incl. VAT) fee plus clearing outstanding dues, subject to seat availability.
