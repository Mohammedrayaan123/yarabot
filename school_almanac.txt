ABOUT THE SCHOOL
----------------------------------------------------------
Yara International School (Y.I.S.) is a CBSE-affiliated school located in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
The school follows the Indian curriculum and is affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi.
The school caters to students from Kindergarten (NUR, LKG, UKG) through Grade XII.
School motto: "We want your Kid to be a Star!"

----------------------------------------------------------
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------
Q: When does the academic year start?
A: The academic year 2026-27 starts in late March/early April 2026. Grade X & XII start on 4th March, Grade IV-XI on 5th April, Grade I-III on 7th April, and Kindergarten (UKG) on 7th April, NUR & LKG on 12th April.

Q: When is summer vacation?
A: Summer vacation is from 5th July to 20th August 2026.

Q: When is winter vacation?
A: Winter vacation is from 20th December to 31st December 2026.

Q: When are the Ramadan holidays?
A: Ramadan holidays are from 10th March to 22nd March 2026, and tentatively 28th February to 11th March 2027 (subject to lunar calendar).

Q: When are the Hajj holidays?
A: Hajj holidays are from 24th May to 2nd June 2026.

Q: When is Teacher's Day?
A: Teacher's Day is celebrated on 5th September, with a celebration on 3rd September 2026.

Q: When is Report Card Day?
A: Report Card Day is on 31st March 2027 for most grades. Grade IX & XI Report Card Day is 18th March 2027.

Q: What is the minimum attendance required?
A: The minimum attendance required is 75%. Students below 75% may not be permitted to sit examinations.

Q: When is Sports Day/Annual Sports Meet?
A: The Annual Sports Meet is on 9th January 2027.

Q: When is the Open House?
A: Open House is on 24th October 2026.

Q: When are Saudi National Day holidays?
A: Saudi National Day extended holidays are on 23rd September 2026 (some grades also include 24th September).

Q: When is Saudi Founding Day?
A: Saudi Founding Day is on 22nd February 2027.

Q: When is Republic Day?
A: Republic Day is on 26th January 2027, with celebrations on 25th January.
